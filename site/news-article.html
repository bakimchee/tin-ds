Flood defences overwhelmed as river hits record level

More than 400 homes were evacuated overnight as the river passed its 1947 peak, with rest centres opening in two schools.

By Sam ReevesEnvironment correspondentLeeds

Published 22 September 2026Updated 18 minutes ago4 min read
ShareShare on FacebookShare on XShare on WhatsAppShare by email

The river passed its 1947 peak just after midnight, overtopping barriers that were finished only last year.

More than 400 homes were evacuated, and rest centres opened in two schools. The council said about 400 people arrived overnight.

At a glance

  • The river peaked at 5.2m, the highest since records began
  • More than 400 homes have been evacuated
  • Three flood barriers were overtopped overnight
  • Rail services are suspended until Wednesday

What went wrong

The defences were designed for a one-in-a-hundred-year flood. This is the third time in a decade that the river has come close to that level.

We have never seen water rise this quickly

Dani OkaforEnvironment Agency

The Environment Agency said it would review the design standard for the barriers before the winter.
